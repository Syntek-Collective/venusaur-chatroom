# Code of Conduct

Venusaur Chatroom started as a learning project, and we want it to stay a welcoming place to learn. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews, or anywhere else in this project) is expected to follow this code of conduct.

## Our Standards

Do:

- be kind, patient, and respectful, especially with people who are new to coding or to open source
- give feedback about the code, not the person
- assume good intent, and ask questions when something is unclear
- accept constructive feedback gracefully

Don't:

- harass, insult, or demean anyone, for any reason
- use sexualized language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances
- publish anyone's private information without their permission
- keep arguing after someone has asked you to stop

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainers. Use the repository's private reporting form ([**Security** tab](https://github.com/Syntek-Collective/venusaur-chatroom/security) → **Report a vulnerability**) and start the title with "Conduct report". Only the maintainers can see it.

We will review every report, keep it as confidential as we can, and respond fairly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behavior is harmful.
